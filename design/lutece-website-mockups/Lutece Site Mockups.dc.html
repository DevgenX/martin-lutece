Ilse Varrow

“I did not come because I was called. I came because no one else would.”

Overview

[Placeholder entry text] Ilse Varrow is the third of the Eight to answer the Summons and the only one to arrive alone. A former archivist of Halcyon Reach, she keeps the record the others would rather lose. Her chapters open the novel.

History

[Placeholder] Born in the lower terraces of the Reach during the long quiet. Trained under the Tithe-keepers; expelled for reasons the archive leaves blank. Sixteen years unaccounted for before the Summons.

In the book

[Placeholder spoiler content] Ilse’s discovery in the Ninth Chair reframes the entire Summons: the Eight were not called, they were recalled. Her final chapter is written in the second person for the first time in the novel.
